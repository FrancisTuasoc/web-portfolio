About Me

Hello! I'm Francis Tuasoc, a senior graphic designer with a passion for crafting bold, purposeful visuals that leave a lasting impression. With years of experience spanning branding, digital media, and UI/UX design, I bring ideas to life through clean composition and intentional design choices.

I believe that great design is more than aesthetics — it's about solving problems, telling stories, and creating meaningful connections between brands and people. Every project I take on is an opportunity to push creative boundaries while staying true to the message at its core.

Outside the studio, I'm always on the move — drawing inspiration from travel, culture, and the everyday world around me. My goal is to keep growing, keep creating, and one day take my work — and myself — wherever I want to go.
